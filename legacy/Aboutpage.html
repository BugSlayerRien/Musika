About Page
Musika, A webpage made to serve and empower Original Pilipino Music (OPM) to people and make Artists be recognized even more, living out the Filipino culture.
This webpage contains only Filipino Artists and Bands ranging from the 90's up to present, you could consider it as an exclusive made for our people to listen and vibe out to especially the kind of melodies and feelings that is portrayed to music.
This webpage is still on development and currently has 15 Artists included. We hope to help our own music to stand out and so our local music scene will be alive and never forgetting our singers and instrument players that produced these music with passion.
